Before You Book

Do I need previous hiking experience?

No. We offer adventures for different experience levels. Each trip clearly states its difficulty so you can choose one that matches your fitness and experience.

What equipment should I bring?

After booking, you'll receive a complete packing list with recommended clothing, footwear and equipment for your chosen destination.

Are flights included?

Flights are not included unless otherwise stated in the trip description. We'll provide guidance on recommended arrival airports and travel arrangements.

How large are the groups?

We keep our groups small to create a more personal experience and reduce our impact on the natural environment.
